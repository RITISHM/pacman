MEET THE GHOST SQUAD

Each ghost has its own unique behavior in the maze!

BLINKY (RED)

The aggressive pursuer. Blinky relentlessly tracks Pac-Man's exact location across the maze.

PINKY (PINK)

The clever ambusher. Pinky tries to position ahead of Pac-Man to cut off escape routes.

INKY (CYAN)

The unpredictable ghost. Inky uses complex tactical movement dependent on Blinky's position.

CLYDE (ORANGE)

The shy roamer. Clyde chases Pac-Man until getting close, then retreats back to his corner.

HOW TO PLAY

Master the arcade controls and eat all the dots to clean the maze!

⌨️ CONTROLS

Move Up: W / ↑ Move Down: S / ↓ Move Left: A / ← Move Right: D / →

🏆 GAME OBJECTIVES

• 🟡 Eat Pac-Dots: Clear all dots from the maze to score points.
• 👻 Avoid Ghosts: Contact with any ghost will force a respawn.
• 🌀 Tunnel Escape: Use side tunnels on left/right to wrap around the map!
• ⭐ High Score: Aim to top the high score board.
